Hydropower Generation and Performance Analysis Using Python - Developed
an educational Python simulation for hydroelectric power generation
using Google Colab. - Analyzed the effects of inflow, hydraulic head,
ecological flow release, turbine capacity and penstock losses on energy
production. - Implemented Darcy–Weisbach friction-loss and minor-loss
calculations and estimated monthly and annual energy generation. -
Compared selected penstock diameters and turbine flow capacities through
a discrete design sweep. Tools: Python, Google Colab, NumPy, Pandas,
